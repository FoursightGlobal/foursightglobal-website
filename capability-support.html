Engagement models

Three ways to work with us

Depending on your requirements, we provide individual specialists, shared service arrangements or dedicated offshore teams that operate as an integrated extension of your existing workforce.

Option 1

Individual specialist

One skilled professional, matched to a defined role, working directly within your processes.

Option 2

Shared service

A shared team handling a defined function, such as drafting, document control or IT support, for one or more of your business areas.

Option 3

Dedicated team

An offshore team that operates as an integrated extension of your workforce, with its own leadership and ongoing accountability.

Beyond lower cost

Lower cost is only the starting point

Successful offshore delivery depends on more than access to lower-cost resources. Effective communication, quality management, accountability and alignment with your expectations are what make the outcome sustainable.

We set up reliable, well-managed support arrangements that give you flexibility and continuity, so your business can focus on its core activities while drawing on specialist capability when it needs it.
